About Gary

Gary the Square Bear is a little cub with a big imagination and an even bigger heart. He may be shaped a little differently, but Gary reminds us all that being unique is something to celebrate. Whether he’s daydreaming on the swing or reading with a smile, Gary brings joy to everyone he meets.
